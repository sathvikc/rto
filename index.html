    Tap the big button when you arrive and when you leave. Forgot? Use Set the time under the button, or tap the "Started …" line to correct it. Every punch can be undone from the message that pops up.

    A shift still open from an earlier day asks when you left, so a forgotten clock-out never becomes a 20-hour day.

    While you're on the clock, Home shows when to leave for three goals (now, on pace for the month, a full day), with today's timesheet hours and what your month average becomes for each. A tip says when the next quarter hour gets credited.

    Office counts toward presence and your average (work + PTO). For a partial day off, keep Office and add the PTO hours.

    OOO and Sick: full day out. They count as present but aren't part of your average. WFH: not present, not in your average.

    Your punches are a prediction until the official timesheet confirms them. Unverified office days show *.

    The timesheet comes out on Wednesday and covers through the previous Friday. Home then shows "days to check". Tap Matches? on a day when the timesheet agrees (tap again to undo), or Edit to enter what it really says. WFH, OOO and Sick days need no check.

    History → Verified only shows the numbers from confirmed days alone, to compare with the official dashboard.

    Times round to a 15-minute grid. Custom rounds down when you're at most "grace" minutes past the quarter, otherwise up. With a 4-minute grace, 4:16–4:19 counts as 4:15 and 4:20 counts as 4:30. The Rounding check below compares predictions with verified days and suggests a grace that fits.

    Tracking month puts a week that straddles two months in the earlier month (a week starting Nov 29 belongs to November), like the timesheet. Calendar month uses each day's own month.

    Company holidays are days in your history, type Holiday. Add them in Settings (copy last year's and adjust the dates), or set any day's type to Holiday. They don't count as workdays. A holiday on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before, one on a Sunday on the Monday after; the list shows the real date with the observed day under it.

    Presence = present days ÷ weekdays in the weeks you logged, not counting company holidays. "Office days left" skips weekends, holidays and days already marked WFH, OOO or Sick.

    Everything stays on this device: there's no account or server. Export a backup now and then. Import merges by date, and days in the file replace the same dates here.
